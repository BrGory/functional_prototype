Bearings have been converted from degrees to radians (0 to ≈6.283):
0° = 0 rad → points to the right (east).
90° = π/2 rad → points straight up (north).
180° = π rad → points left (west).
270° = 3π/2 → points straight down (south).
360° = 2π rad → full rotation, back to east.
