An observation index was used because not every contiguous day had a reading for SAR image drift and buoy drift. The index allows for a transition between each observation. The distance delta shows how far apart are the observation locations in km.


The red line represents normal distribution. The blue dots are the residuals (error distance from the regression line). For normally distributed errors, we would blue points close to the red line for the entire residual set. However, we see "tails" at the bottom and top of the red line. This indicates the central resiudals are normally distubuted, but high and low values do not match that expected distribution. This could be an indication that the values for either the buoys are SAR image need some correction before fitting a regression for the relationship of the two data sets.
